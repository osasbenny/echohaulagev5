# Delivery Roadmap, Backlog and Ownership

**Owner:** Cactus Digital Media · **Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Status:** proposed; not evidence of deployed features.

## October 22–31 — P0 baseline
Review v5 and earlier source, verify clean install/build and dependencies, document secrets/sensitive tracked files, freeze schema and roles, confirm carrier business agreements, pilot area, commission, provider and architecture decisions; create test environment and tasks.

## November 1–15 — Organizations and quote marketplace
Build courier applications/admin approval, merchant accounts, tenant memberships, service zones/rate cards, filtered provider quotes and authorization tests.

## November 16–30 — Shipments and fulfillment
Implement locked bookings, authorized dispatch, driver assignment, event-based timeline, recipient-safe tracking, pickup and private POD, retries/alerts and disputes.

## December 1–10 — Payments and reporting
NGN gateway, signed verification, webhook dedupe, commission ledger, refunds and finance-reviewed vendor payout statements; reconcile synthetic and test-mode transactions.

## December 11–20 — Verification and pilot
Role/tenant tests, failure injection, security review, merchant API, selected courier pilot, accessible mobile workflow, operations training, backup restore.

## December 21–31 — Conditional go/no-go
Fix all P0 defects; approve legal terms and merchant/carrier contracts; validate booking→POD→settlement end-to-end; release limited supported zones if signed off. Dates targets not guaranteed.

P0 = integrity and fulfillment; P1 = analytics and commerce connectors; P2 = GPS, global shipping, COD and native mobile. Founder approves release; engineering, operations and finance owners to be assigned rather than fabricated.
